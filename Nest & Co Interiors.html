FAQ

Good to know

Answers to what clients ask most.

Is the site visit and quote free?

Yes. We visit your home, take measurements and share a detailed quote at no cost.

How long does a full-home project take?

Most 2 and 3 BHK homes take 45 to 75 days from design approval. We share a clear timeline before we begin.

What is the starting budget?

Modular kitchens and wardrobes can start at a few lakh rupees, depending on size and materials. Share your needs for an accurate quote.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We give a written warranty on workmanship and pass on the hardware and material brand warranties.

Can I make changes during the project?

Small changes are welcome before production starts. We will explain any change in cost or time before going ahead.
